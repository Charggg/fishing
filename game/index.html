❔  How to fish

The loop

  1. Aim and hold left click. The power meter fills — release to cast. Aim slightly above the water; the line arcs.
  2. Wait. The bobber sits. Fish that like your lure, at that depth, at that hour, come to look.
  3. Watch the bobber. Small dips are a fish tasting it. When it plunges and the screen shouts at you, click.
  4. Fight it. Hold left click to reel. That loads the line. Let go to give slack when it runs, or the line snaps.

Reading the fight

The tension bar is the only thing that can kill you. Pinned in the red for about half a second and the line parts. When a fish is running, stop reeling. When it is giving, wind like mad. Drag (mouse wheel) trades speed for safety: high drag gains line faster but loads the rod harder.

What makes fish bite

  • Depth. Each lure fishes at its own depth. A deep jig will never find a bluegill in the reeds.
  • Time. Walleye and brown trout are night fish. Bass hunt at dawn and dusk. Some things only exist after midnight.
  • Weather. Rain and storms bring out the big ones. Fog hides the rare stuff.
  • Where you cast. There is a deep hole out to the north-east and a weed flat to the south-west. They hold different fish.
